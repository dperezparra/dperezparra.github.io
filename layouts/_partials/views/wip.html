This is an ADDITION, not an override - the theme has no views/wip.html, and `functions/render_view` resolves views by name, so nothing upstream is shadowed. Why not the built-in `citation` view: it hard-codes an APA-style year "(2026)." and trails the entry with the `publication` string, neither of which suits a list of unpublished projects. It also opens with a large `mt-16 sm:mt-20` gap under the block title.
